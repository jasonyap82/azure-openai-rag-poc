# Contoso Cloud Services - Billing and Subscriptions

## Billing cycle

Invoices are generated on the first day of each calendar month for the preceding
month's usage. Payment is due 30 days from the invoice date. Accounts more than 45
days past due are suspended automatically; suspension is lifted within one business
hour of payment clearing.

## Cancellation and proration

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time and are prorated to the day of
cancellation. Annual subscriptions are not prorated on cancellation, but unused
months may be converted to account credit at 80% of their face value if the request
is made within the first six months of the term.

## Reserved capacity

Reserved capacity commitments are available in one-year and three-year terms and
discount on-demand rates by 32% and 54% respectively. Reservations apply
automatically to matching workloads in the same region and cannot be moved between
regions after purchase.

Reservations may be exchanged once per term for a reservation of equal or greater
value. Refunds on reservations are capped at 50,000 USD per subscription per rolling
twelve-month period.

## Cost management

Budget alerts can be configured at the subscription or resource-group level and fire
at 50%, 80%, 90%, and 100% of the configured threshold. Alerts are notifications
only; they do not stop resource consumption. Hard spending caps are available on
sandbox subscription types only.

## Support plans

Developer support is included at no cost and offers business-hours response within
one business day. Standard support costs 100 USD per month and offers a four-hour
response for production-down incidents. Premier support is quoted individually and
offers a fifteen-minute response with a named technical account manager.
