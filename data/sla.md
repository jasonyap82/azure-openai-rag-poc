# Contoso Cloud Services - Service Level Agreement

## Availability commitment

Contoso Compute and Contoso Managed Database carry a monthly uptime commitment of
99.95% for Standard tier and 99.99% for Premium tier. Uptime is measured per region
per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72
hours in advance.

Contoso Object Storage carries a 99.9% commitment on Standard tier. Storage does not
offer a Premium tier at this time.

## Claiming service credits

Customers must file a credit claim within 30 calendar days of the end of the month in
which the incident occurred. Claims filed after this window are not eligible for
review, regardless of incident severity. File claims through the support portal using
claim type SLA-4402.

Credits are applied against the following month's invoice. Credits are never issued
as cash refunds and cannot be transferred between subscriptions or accounts.

## Credit schedule

For Standard tier, monthly uptime below 99.95% but at or above 99.0% earns a 10%
credit. Uptime below 99.0% but at or above 95.0% earns a 25% credit. Uptime below
95.0% earns a 100% credit of that month's fees for the affected service.

For Premium tier, monthly uptime below 99.99% but at or above 99.9% earns a 15%
credit. Uptime below 99.9% but at or above 99.0% earns a 30% credit. Uptime below
99.0% earns a 100% credit.

## Exclusions

The SLA does not apply to downtime caused by customer configuration errors, use of
preview or beta features, suspension for non-payment, or factors outside Contoso's
reasonable control including denial-of-service attacks originating from the
customer's own workloads.

Preview features are explicitly excluded from all availability commitments and are
provided without warranty of any kind.
